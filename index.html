After you download

Setup vs Portable

Setup installs like a normal app and updates in place (Windows). Portable keeps everything — app, archive, settings — in a data folder next to the exe; move or delete that folder and the whole thing goes with it.

About the security warning

The builds are not code-signed yet. Windows SmartScreen: click "More info", then "Run anyway". macOS: right-click the app and choose "Open" the first time.

First steps

  1. Run the app.
  2. In the Sync menu, choose "Open TikTok window" and sign in.
  3. Choose "Sync now". Your liked, favorited, and followed-creator videos start archiving locally.
